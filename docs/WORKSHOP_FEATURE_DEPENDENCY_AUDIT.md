# CubeGears — Workshop feature dependency audit

## Scope and truth source
Checked the Django tenant permission catalogue, the Company Panel's actual
routes, workshop onboarding and credit-ledger services, and the payroll daily
wage and attendance services. This document records products whose components
are already backed by implemented APIs and permissions; it does not invent
features, calculations, customer data or payment integrations.

## Tenant access and activation contract

| Single paid product | Included existing API modules | Existing workflow | Data dependencies |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Workshop Operations | customers, vehicles, services, jobs, stock, invoices, notifications | Customer → vehicle → job card → service / stock movement → estimate → invoice → customer update | Supplier management and purchases are already part of Inventory; parts, invoice and job routes still require their existing role permissions |
| Accounts & Tax | payments, expenses, finance, gstr | Invoice / supplier purchase / wage payment → cash, bank or UPI transaction → accounts ledger → GST review and returns | Financial reports read actual underlying invoice/purchase/payment entries; some records require Workshop Operations data |
| Attendance & Payroll | attendance, payroll | Shift → punch/attendance and leave → approved overtime → finalized daily wages or payroll → approval and payment | The implemented payroll service reads AttendanceRecord, LeaveRequest, OvertimeRequest and rates; a purchased feature **does not** auto-approve attendance or payments |
| Reports & Analytics | reports | Data generated by accessible modules → authorized reports | A report never bypasses another module's underlying data permissions |
| Storage | storage | Track real upload bytes → monthly storage usage and ledger | Independent storage metering; owner can read storage from SaaS Account. Not included in a per-user bundle |

The **free** tenant modules stay Dashboard, Settings, Staff Management,
Roles/Permissions and SaaS Billing. Platform `company` is never sold as
a product. The `finance.accounts.balance.view` permission maps to the
Accounts & Tax purchase **but remains role-protected**.

### Pricing and legacy handling

- One new purchase record for each workflow package: **5 credits per active
  login user per published billing period** initially; SaaS platform pricing
  controls can change future prices after existing usage is checkpointed.
- No purchase/grant is made merely by running a migration or creating an
  account; an empty wallet has **0** real credits.
- Old standalone attendance/payroll/job/invoice/etc. labels are still usable
  until their existing terms expire. New customers are shown integrated
  packages instead of single overlapping labels.
- New grouped purchases are blocked if a related old prepaid, postpaid or
  active metered entitlement remains. Billing/support must explicitly settle,
  credit or migrate that prior entitlement rather than charging twice.
- Groups affect paid entitlement only; `user_has_permission` independently
  enforces company tenancy, employee role and permission codes. No new
  cross-tenant access, forged invoices or automatic payroll payment.
- Legacy custom bundles spanning multiple groups remain in their original
  records; platform staff must audit their business terms before replacing
  or consolidating them.

### First-signup contract

A newly registered owner has `onboarding.required=true` and one
zero-credit wallet. Company Panel displays only the four-step onboarding,
without sidebar, header or mobile bottom navigation. Company and branch
identity/contact/address fields are required, with operations and billing
defaults as separate steps. GST registration must be a valid GSTIN **or** the
explicit `gstNotRegistered` opt-out. GPS, logo and email remain optional
for mobile-only or unregistered workshops. Django rejects completion if any
saved mandatory values are absent, and tenant RBAC rejects operational actions
during setup. Old workshops with no required onboarding flag remain unchanged.
After completion, re-authentication loads permissions and the owner goes to
`/account` for current wallet balance, credit top-ups and package activation.

  (Vercel projects previously linked to older/different GitHub repositories).
- Configure an actual PostgreSQL host, Redis/Celery worker and environment

  incomplete contact fields on a deployed backend.
- Run complete workshop job to invoice to customer approval to payment
  settlement, and supplier purchase/return workflows with test data.
- Run real attendance, overtime approval, wage calculation, corrections,
  bank payout, reversal and accounting reports with signed-in workshop roles.

  configuration; CI cannot claim live message delivery.
- Configure wildcard DNS + HTTPS for default customer websites, then test
  tenant isolation and one verified workshop-owned custom domain.
