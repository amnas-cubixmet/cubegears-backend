Settlement timeline

  1. Month end: freeze recorded feature seconds and storage GB-time.
  2. Next month {{ billing_cycle_policy.due_day|default:5 }}: automatically deduct credits if balance covers the entire statement.
  3. After due day: unpaid companies retain sign-in, account, recharge and storage audit; workshop pages are denied if restriction is enabled.

Activation month: {{ billing_cycle_policy.effective_month|default:"Set on first enable" }}. No pre-activation period is automatically charged.
